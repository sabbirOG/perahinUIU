📖 Project Story

Perahin UIU started as a personal project to organize my own notes and resources. As a UIU student, I often found it challenging to access academic materials and tools all in one place. That’s why I decided to build this platform.

It began with a CGPA calculator that accurately follows UIU’s grading system, and over time added course playlists, study materials, and more. Today, Perahin UIU supports the entire student community, and I continue to enhance it based on feedback and real student needs.

FAQ

How do I access course materials or playlists?
You can find course materials and playlists in the navigation menu. Just click on "Course Materials" or "PlayList" to explore resources for your subjects.
Can I contribute resources or feedback?
Yes! Student contributions are highly encouraged. Share your resources, ideas, or feedback using the contact options on this page to help make Perahin UIU better for everyone.
Who developed Perahin UIU?
Perahin UIU was created by a UIU student to help fellow students access resources and tools more easily. Follow the GitHub for more updates.
How do I stay updated with new features?
Check the platform regularly for updates and new features. Major changes are announced on the dashboard or via notices.
