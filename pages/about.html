📖 Project Story

Perahin UIU began as a simple collection of my personal notes and resources. As a UIU student, I struggled to find organized academic materials and tools in one place. So I built this platform - starting with a CGPA calculator that actually follows UIU's grading system, then adding course playlists, study materials, and more. Today, it serves the entire UIU student community, and I continue to improve it based on student feedback.
